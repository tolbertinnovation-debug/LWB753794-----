MORE ROOM FOR YOUR IDEAS

Tools to move you forward.

01

Learn with focus

Read articles without clutter, compare pages in split view and keep useful pages in your reading list.

02

Keep projects together

Save groups of tabs as workspaces and return to your research, lessons and projects when you are ready.

03

Take privacy seriously

Built-in ad and tracker blocking, private windows, HTTPS-Only controls and site permission prompts put choices in your hands.

04

Share what matters

Save pages as PDFs, capture screenshots and copy clean links or a group of open tab links.

05

Manage busy days

Find tabs through the command palette, use vertical tabs and let eligible background tabs sleep to free memory.

06

Make it yours

Choose your theme, background, search engine and shortcuts. Bring bookmarks from your existing browser.

Features depend on the installed version. Check the latest release notes for availability. No built-in VPN tunnel is included.
